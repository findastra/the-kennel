# the-kennel

**Pets made by other people, for [Astra's PetOS](https://github.com/findastra/astras-pet-os).** Bring a pet you built;
other farmers try it, review it, and may adopt it onto their own farm.

*A public place of Astra's PetOS, by Astra.* Its neighbours: the [vet-clinic](https://github.com/findastra/vet-clinic)
(sick pets) and [the-shed](https://github.com/findastra/the-shed) (tools and tricks).

## Bring a pet to the Kennel

Use **New issue → Submit a pet**. A pet is a pet program (its dashboard, built to the
[Pet Program Framework](https://github.com/findastra/astras-pet-os/blob/main/PET-PROGRAM-FRAMEWORK.md)) plus its
icon. Say what job it does, which programs and tools it uses, which permissions it needs (folders, and which of
R W X), and link its source and license. Use versioned names: `<pet-program>-v<version>`.

## ⚠️ Every pet here is a stranger's pet until your farm adopts it

> **Community pet.** Made by someone you do not know. Your farm never runs it as it is: it goes into quarantine, a
> checker agent reviews it, and your farm's L4 (the verified human who owns it) approves it first. Only then does it
> become an animal on your farm.

Trust tiers, highest first: **official** (signed by the maintainers), **Kennel-verified** (reviewed and adopted
through the Kennel), **community** (everything else, always with the warning above).

Farms never connect to each other. This repo is a shared shelf, nothing more: farms pull from it, it never pushes into a farm.
